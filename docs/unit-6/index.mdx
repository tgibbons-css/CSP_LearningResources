## Interactive activities

Use the [five-team synthesis builder](./why-we-need-data-centers.mdx#in-class-collaborative-synthesis), then the [Hermantown jigsaw](./hermantown-data-center-case-study.mdx#how-the-jigsaw-works). The synthesis builder saves drafts locally. For the Hermantown jigsaw, write directly in your assigned shared Google Docs.

## Assignments

Complete your assigned pod and team work in the [Hermantown jigsaw](./hermantown-data-center-case-study.mdx#how-the-jigsaw-works) and contribute to the final class decision. Refer to your instructor's Brightspace instructions for submission details.
