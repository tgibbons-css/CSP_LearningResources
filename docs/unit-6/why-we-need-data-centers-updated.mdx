## Why do we need data centers?

A spreadsheet might fit on one laptop. Streaming video, serving millions of users, or training AI models can require many computers working together. Those computers need buildings, power, networks, and cooling.

Using more machines is not the only solution: efficient software, reduced workloads, and shared resources can also lower infrastructure needs. [What is the cloud? (Cloudflare)](https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/cloud/what-is-the-cloud/)

## The five V’s of Big Data

- **Volume:** How much data is there?
- **Velocity:** How fast does data arrive or need to be processed?
- **Variety:** What kinds of data are involved?
- **Veracity:** How accurate and trustworthy is the data?
- **Value:** What useful result does the data provide?

## Two related concepts

**Data formats:** Spreadsheets contain structured tables; JSON represents information using keys and values; text, images, and audio often need different methods of processing. The format alone does not determine how much storage is needed.

**Resources:** [AWS: Structured vs. Unstructured Data](https://aws.amazon.com/compare/the-difference-between-structured-data-and-unstructured-data/) · [MDN: JSON](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development/Core/Scripting/JSON) · [Video: Data Types](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eK3I6oI_4Is)

**Distributed computing:** Multiple networked computers share processing and storage. This increases capacity but also introduces networking, communication, and coordination challenges.

**Resources:** [AWS: Cloud Computing](https://aws.amazon.com/what-is-cloud-computing/) · [AWS: Distributed Computing](https://aws.amazon.com/what-is/distributed-computing/) · [IBM: Distributed Systems](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/distributed-systems)
