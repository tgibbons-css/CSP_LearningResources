## How the jigsaw works

Your instructor will assign you to **one expert pod (1–4)** and **one home team (1–5)**. Research with your pod, teach your home team, then help the class reach a decision. Record your work directly in the shared Google Docs below; their prompts and tables guide each phase.

All document and resource links open in a new tab. You can also browse the <a href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1NwGRsUHZnrEJSWnUkTzCvnrnxK70FGps" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hermantown activity folder</a>.

### Phase 1: Expert pods

Open **your assigned pod document**. Add your name and home-team number, then use its questions and sources to complete the research table together. Record source links and dates, identify uncertainty, and prepare **three key findings and one proposed enforceable safeguard**. Each member should be ready to teach these to their home team in a two-minute briefing.

### Phase 2: Home teams

Move to **your assigned home team**, bringing perspectives from all four pods. Open only your team's workspace below and record members' names and expert roles.

1. **Share evidence (Part A):** Each expert briefs the team on findings, sources, uncertainty, and a recommended safeguard.
2. **Weigh trade-offs (Part B):** Compare benefits, costs, and risks across all four areas.
3. **Make a recommendation (Part C):** Choose whether the project should proceed as proposed, proceed only with additional binding conditions, or not proceed based on available evidence. Support your reasoning with evidence from at least three expert areas.
4. **Propose conditions (Part D):** Draft up to four conditions addressing energy, environment/water, economy, and community. Specify a measurable standard, responsible party, monitoring/enforcement, and consequence. Flag authority or standards that need verification.
5. **Prepare your report (Part E):** Summarize your recommendation, strongest evidence, most important condition, and one unresolved question in two minutes.

### Phase 3: Final class decision

Each team presents its recommendation and strongest condition. As directed by your instructor, use <a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1jJoYD6ZhIN-82ijr7WsgHUQiVnub93_hiD5e5_0vZRI/edit?usp=drivesdk" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Final Decision — Hermantown Data Center: Binding Policy Conditions</a> to record the class recommendation and up to four agreed conditions. Include supporting evidence, remaining disagreements, and questions needing more information. Explain any omitted condition.
