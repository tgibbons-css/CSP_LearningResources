### Reading the estimator: What does PUE mean?

**PUE stands for Power Usage Effectiveness.** It compares the energy used by the entire data center with the energy used by its information technology (IT) equipment, such as servers, storage, and networking equipment.

**PUE = total facility energy ÷ IT equipment energy**, measured over the same period. PUE is a ratio with no units.

For example, a **PUE of 1.25** means the facility uses **1.25 kWh for every 1 kWh used by IT equipment**. The extra **0.25 kWh** supports cooling, power conversion, and other facility systems. With a constant 100 MW IT load, that ratio corresponds to 125 MW of total facility power and 25 MW of overhead.

A PUE closer to **1.0** means less facility overhead per unit of IT energy. It does not tell us how useful the computing is, how much water is used, or whether the electricity comes from clean sources. Use PUE alongside the water estimates when comparing cooling choices.

## From One Day to a Full Year: Seasonal Cooling Trade-offs

The estimator above compares cooling choices under one set of conditions. But a data center operates throughout the year. How might Minnesota's changing temperatures affect electricity demand and water use?

In the <a href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gdclunNC525hWhev2urzdnCYQTotwaCOqqSjB1kAcwU/edit?usp=sharing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hermantown Seasonal Cooling Spreadsheet</a>, you will compare evaporative cooling and air cooling across 12 months. The spreadsheet uses illustrative assumptions, not measured Hermantown operating data.

### Before you begin

Discuss these questions as a class:

- Why might cooling electricity and water use change between January and July?
- How does **power** in MW become monthly **energy** in MWh?
- Does reducing on-site water use necessarily reduce electricity use?

Trace the spreadsheet's completed January example:

| Calculation | Relationship |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly IT energy | IT load in MW × 24 × days in month |
| Total facility energy | IT energy × PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness) |
| Facility energy overhead | Total facility energy − IT energy |
| Evaporative water use | IT energy in MWh × 1,000 × WUE in L/kWh × gallons per liter |

**Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE)** is the ratio of total facility energy to IT energy. Multiply monthly IT energy by PUE to include facility overhead. **Water Usage Effectiveness (WUE)** measures water use per unit of IT energy. In the water calculation, multiplying MWh by 1,000 converts to kWh before applying WUE. Facility overhead includes cooling and other facility systems.

The spreadsheet uses `IF()` to select the air-cooling **Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE)** from temperature conditions. A higher PUE means more facility energy for the same IT energy. As you extend formulas, notice which references change by month and which constants stay fixed using `$`, such as `$B$5`.
